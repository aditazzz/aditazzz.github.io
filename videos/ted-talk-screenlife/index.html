def render_frame(t, W=1920, H=1080, fi=0):
    vert = H > W
    S = state(t, vert)
    cam = make_cam(t, W, H)
    img = np.zeros((H, W, 3), F32)
    AP.desktop(img, cam, S)
    AP.desktop_items(img, cam, S)
    AP.huddle(img, cam, S)
    AP.notes(img, cam, S)
    AP.calendar(img, cam, S)
    for d in S.dialogs: AP.dialog(img, cam, S, d)
    for m in S.menus: AP.menu(img, cam, S, m)
    if S.menus and S.menus[0]["k"] > 0.5 and t > T_NOEND + 0.2:
        AP.tooltip(img, cam, S, B_LEAVE[0], B_LEAVE[1] - 22 - 16 - 2 * 40 - 4, "Only Ted can end it", 0)
    AP.dm_window(img, cam, S)
    AP.menubar(img, cam, S)
    AP.dock(img, cam, S)
    AP.notifications(img, cam, S)
    U.cursor(img, cam, S.cursor[0], S.cursor[1], 1.0, 1.0 - S.lid, S.click)
    # the lid closes: the screen goes dark
    if S.lid > 0:
        img *= 1 - S.lid
        yy = np.linspace(0, 1, H, dtype=F32)[:, None, None]
        img += (0.06 * S.lid * (1 - S.lid) * 4) * (1 - yy) * np.array([0.6, 0.7, 1.0], F32)
    lyrics(img, t, W, H)
    if not C.STATE["clean"]:
        C.subscribe_pill(img, t, W, H, T_PILL, 5.0)
        C.end_screen(img, t, W, H, T_END, DUR - T_END, "It's always a Ted talk.", sub_line="Subscribe for a new song every week",
                     title="TED Talk, screenlife", accent=U.hexc("e0352b"))
    return finish(img, t, fi)

What you see

The whole song plays out on my laptop screen, like a screenlife film. The standup is a video call and Ted is the host. My question sits in the chat while he answers it with a question, and four minutes in the meeting timer says it all. At "so — stepping back" he literally steps back from his webcam, somebody dims the lights (dark mode), everybody sits down (cameras off), and his virtual background turns into a keynote stage. He shares eighteen minutes of blank slides, a photo of his father fishing, a loading circle for circling back and a picture of himself at seven in the rain. I keep a tally of every "so" and every "right?", get buzzword bingo, and watch my calendar crash. At "Ted. This is a standup." I unmute: eight people, fifteen minutes, Ted has used six, and Dana, who left two years ago, is a ghost in her tile now. Nobody can end the meeting, the host has turned off Q&A, and when he finally takes it offline, my message to him can't be delivered.
