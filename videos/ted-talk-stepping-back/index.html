The code behind it

Every frame of this video is drawn by a Python program. This is the heart of it, from scene.py, line 135.

def render_frame(t, W=1920, H=1080, fi=0):
    vert = H > W
    z = Z(t); th = ROT(t); st = story(t)
    mpp = 10 ** z / 1920.0
    out = None; k_space = float(sstep(6.15, 6.55, z))
    if k_space < 1:
        view = View(W, H, *map_center(W, H), mpp, th)
        cvm = CM.draw_map(view, t, z, st)
        img_map = cvm.astype(F32) / 255.0
        lights(img_map, view, t, z)
        if z > 5.15:
            col, a = CM.clouds(view, cloud_cover(z))
            img_map = img_map * (1 - a[..., None]) + col * a[..., None]
        out = img_map
    if k_space > 0:
        img_sp = render_space(t, z, W, H, fi)
        out = img_sp if out is None else out * (1 - k_space) + img_sp * k_space
    overlays(out, t, z, W, H, th, st)
    return finish(out, t, fi)

What you see

One continuous zoom out, every power of ten timed to the song: the standup from the ceiling, the office with its roof off, a city whose streets are named after Ted's buzzwords (Circle Back Blvd, Synergy St, Lake Pause, a town called Right?), the clouds, the Earth while my calendar gives up, the Moon, the planets circling back, the stars, the Milky Way, Andromeda, the cosmic web, and the edge of what anyone can see. At "Ted. This is a standup." it snaps all the way back into the room, where Ted has used 6 of our 15 minutes and Dana has become a ghost.
