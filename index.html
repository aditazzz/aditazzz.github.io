How a video gets made

  1. The song

    I write the lyrics and the concept, then make the track in Suno.

  2. The sync

    The vocals are separated, transcribed and lined up with the lyrics, so every sung word gets a timestamp. Beats and sections come from the audio too.

  3. The scene

    Claude writes one program per song, a function that returns the picture at any moment of the song. I direct it and review every still.

  4. The render

    Every frame is drawn at 1080p, 30 fps, for YouTube and for Shorts, then cut, packaged and uploaded by the same pipeline.

All of it is open source: the pipeline and every scene on GitHub.
